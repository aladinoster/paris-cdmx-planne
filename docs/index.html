    How the optimiser works

    Every candidate trip has a start day, an end day, a place (Paris, Mexico City or a third city) and whether the host takes leave. It is worth V € per day together, × β on days the host works, × ½ on the two travel days, plus a city’s extra €/day. From that we subtract the fares, the hotel and a fixed overhead C. A gap of g days between trips costs λ·max(0, g − target)², so long silences hurt more than proportionally.

    A dynamic programme over the 365 days tracks your remaining leave, her remaining leave and which fixed trips are done. The result is the true optimum for these inputs, not a heuristic. The same model ships as a Python package (meetplan) in the repository, with tests that check the two agree.

    Fares come from a seasonal curve calibrated on Kiwi.com quotes collected in November 2026 for 2027 dates. Third cities are scaled from May and March quotes. Treat every figure as an estimate and use “Fare level” once you see live prices.
